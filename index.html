Robotics and technology in Australia

Four industries, four different paths

Since 1994, Australia's workforce has not changed evenly. Technology is only one reason, but it shows that every industry has been reshaped differently.

Overview

The workforce has not changed evenly

Health care has more than tripled its jobs and mining has nearly tripled its own, while manufacturing and agriculture now employ about a fifth fewer people than they did. In the pages ahead, we look at how technology is working inside each one, from factory floors and mine sites to paddocks and hospital wards.
